## A. BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING

Beyond Fitness is a multi-disciplinary training centre in Manjeri — not a conventional neighbourhood gym selling memberships and machines.

### What it actually is

A place where people train:

| Domain | Expression |
|--------|------------|
| Body | Strength, conditioning, personal training, nutrition |
| Skills | Fencing, kickboxing, martial arts, combat sports |
| Discipline | Habit, form, consistency, coaching culture |
| Performance | Measurable progress, athletic intent, sport readiness |

Public footprint (to be confirmed with client):

- **Address:** UA Mall, Mullampara Rd, Kacherippadi, Manjeri 676121
- **Hours:** roughly 5:30am–11pm (Sunday shorter)
- **Reputation:** high local rating (~4.9)
- **Instagram:** `@beyond_._fitness`
- **Leadership signal:** fencing / Sportster FC connection — combat & sport are not a side note; they are structural to the brand

### Strategic insight

Competitors in Manjeri (and most Indian gym sites) sell **access to equipment**.  
Beyond Fitness should sell **access to a training culture**.

The website’s job is not “list gym amenities.”  
It is to make someone feel: *this is where people who take training seriously go.*

Client screenshots / Instagram references were described but not present as files in the empty repo. Until assets are dropped into the project, we treat this as provisional DNA:

**Do not invent an unrelated palette.** Build from black / white / maroon–red already living in the space.

### Positioning line (working)

> **Beyond Fitness is a training culture — body, skill, discipline, performance.**

Not: “Best gym in Manjeri.”

## C. BRAND OPPORTUNITY

### Category clichés to refuse

- Muscular hero + “Transform Your Body”
- Black/red neon gym template
- Three rounded service cards
- Pricing table as the emotional climax
- Stock sweat photography
- Glowing “Join Now”
- Motivational quote as hero wallpaper
- Soft cream + serif “luxury wellness” (this studio’s prior pattern)

### Local competitive vacuum

Manjeri gym websites (where they exist) tend to be:

- Service lists
- Phone numbers
- Generic “body building / fat loss / zumba” menus
- No brand theatre, no editorial craft, no dual-world storytelling

**Opportunity:** Own the territory of a **premium multi-disciplinary training house** — gym *and* combat/sport under one philosophy — with sports-editorial visual authority that feels impossible to confuse with a franchise gym landing page.

### Opportunity statement

> Build the first digital identity in Manjeri that feels like a **serious athletic institution**, not a membership shop — while remaining clear enough for a WhatsApp-first mobile user.

### Differentiation vs our previous websites

| Previous pattern | Beyond Fitness must do the opposite |
|------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Cream / gold / hospitality warmth | Graphite / black / controlled maroon heat |
| Soft rounded cards, pill CTAs | Sharp geometry, editorial edges, sparse cards |
| Heritage storytelling, product mosaics | Physical training culture, dual arenas |
| Calm wellness / F&B appetite | Controlled intensity, motion-as-force |
| Hero → about → stats → cards → CTA | Structure driven by training logic |

## D. DESIGN CONCEPT OPTIONS

Three genuinely different directions. Not palette swaps.

---

### DIRECTION 01 — “TRAINING DOSSIER”

**Concept**  
The site behaves like a high-end athletic dossier / sports editorial: numbered protocols, cropped action stills, technical captions, progression markers. Beyond Fitness as a documented training institution.

**Emotional feeling**  
Focused. Credible. Sharp. Quietly intense. Like flipping through a coach’s notebook shot by a sports photographer.

**Visual language**  
- Asymmetric editorial grids  
- Oversized controlled typography  
- Tight photographic crops (hands, stance, bar path, blade tip, footwork)  
- Small technical labels: `01 / STRENGTH`, `COORD: MANJERI`, `SESSION`  
- Subtle paper grain / matte photo treatment  
- Horizontal rules as “lanes,” not decoration

**Typography**  
- Display: condensed athletic grotesque (e.g. condensed sans / sports condensed)  
- Body: clean neo-grotesque  
- Mono for labels, times, coordinates  
Avoid: decorative serifs, script, “motivational” italic quotes as brand voice

**Colour**  
- Foundation: near-black + off-white  
- Accent: restrained maroon/red from interiors (not neon)  
- Neutrals: graphite, concrete grey  
Red is punctuation, not wallpaper.

**Photography**  
Real space. Real people. High contrast. Prefer candid training over posed flex. Crop aggressively. Mix gym floor + combat floor in the same visual grammar.

**Layout philosophy**  
Magazine spreads. Large type + one dominant image per section. Negative space as recovery between sets. Not card grids.

**Motion philosophy**  
Reveals like page turns / shutter opens. Type enters with weight. Images mask-reveal on scroll. Short, decisive, never floaty.

**Interaction ideas**  
- Hover: image crop shifts (as if adjusting stance)  
- Section index markers that update as you scroll (“Protocol 03”)  
- Sticky micro-label for current training domain

**Why it fits**  
Elevates beyond “local gym site” into sports-brand territory while staying readable. Works even before combat content is fully confirmed.

**Risks**  
- Can feel magazine-cool but emotionally cold  
- Technical labels can become gimmick if meaningless  
- Needs strong real photography; stock will kill it

---

### DIRECTION 02 — “ONE PHILOSOPHY / TWO ARENAS”

**Concept**  
The site is structured around two visual worlds that are clearly different but share one system:

- **ARENA A — FITNESS** (strength, conditioning, PT, nutrition)  
- **ARENA B — COMBAT / SPORT** (fencing, kickboxing, martial arts)

They are not two businesses. They are two expressions of **Beyond your limits.**

The experience transitions between arenas — same type system, same black/white/maroon DNA, different image temperature and rhythm.

**Emotional feeling**  
Powerful. Distinctive. Memorable. “I’ve never seen a local gym site do this.” Dual intensity under one roof.

**Visual language**  
- Shared: black/white foundation, maroon accent, sharp geometry, editorial type  
- Fitness world: heavier crops, iron, grounding horizontal layouts, denser type  
- Combat world: sharper diagonals, speed, negative space, precision crops (blade, glove, stance)  
- Transition device: a deliberate “threshold” moment (split screen, wipe, or dual-lane scroll) that states the philosophy once

**Typography**  
Same family across both worlds. Differentiation via weight, tracking, and layout density — not different fonts that fracture the brand.

**Colour**  
Shared palette. Possible subtle shift in accent usage:

- Fitness: maroon as heat / effort  
- Combat: maroon as strike / impact (more sparse, more graphic)

Never two unrelated colour systems.

**Photography**  
Paired sequences: strength ↔ skill. Community present in both. Avoid segregating audiences visually (don’t make combat look “male only” or fitness look “generic”).

**Layout philosophy**  
Homepage as a journey through both arenas, then convergence into “How to start.” Deep pages can be arena-specific without feeling like a separate site.

**Motion philosophy**  
Directional force:

- Fitness: vertical grind / press (weight, ascent)  
- Combat: horizontal / diagonal strike (speed, cut)  
Transitions between arenas should feel like crossing into another training floor — intentional, not a theme-park morph.

**Interaction ideas**  
- Dual-lane chooser: “Train the body” / “Train the skill” — both lead deeper into the same brand  
- Hover on arena tiles reveals a single defining verb (PRESS / STRIKE / HOLD / ADVANCE)  
- Scroll threshold: brief locked statement — *Two arenas. One standard.*

**Why it fits**  
This is the brand’s sharpest strategic truth. No Manjeri competitor owns “gym + fencing + combat” as one digital identity. This direction *is* the business model made visible.

**Risks**  
- Dual-world UI can feel gimmicky or confusing if over-designed  
- Parents / gym seekers might bounce if combat appears first without clarity  
- Requires careful IA so WhatsApp users still get answers fast  
- Needs enough real combat + fitness imagery to sustain both worlds

---

### DIRECTION 03 — “KINETIC FIELD”

**Concept**  
Movement itself is the primary design material. Typography, spacing, and scroll behave like physical training: tension, release, repetition, progression. Less “brochure with photos,” more “spatial performance.”

**Emotional feeling**  
Alive. Contemporary. Experimental-premium. Like a performance brand (NikeLab / On / fencing federation editorial) filtered for a local serious gym.

**Visual language**  
- Large kinetic type blocks  
- Directional composition (left→right force, ascending numbers)  
- Abstract geometric “lanes” and impact lines used sparingly  
- Photography as texture layers behind type, often partial  
- Progression meter / rep-count metaphors in UI chrome

**Typography**  
Variable or tightly tracked grotesques that can stretch/condense in motion. Numbers as heroes. Less caption culture than Direction 01; more type-as-body.

**Colour**  
Stark B/W with rare maroon flashes on impact moments (CTA, strike transitions). Almost monochrome by default.

**Photography**  
Secondary to type/motion in some sections; when used, full-bleed and masked. Risk: can underuse the gym’s real atmosphere if over-abstracted.

**Layout philosophy**  
Scroll choreography as narrative. Sections feel like stations in a workout. Mobile becomes a vertical training sequence.

**Motion philosophy**  
Scroll-linked progression is core (not optional). Velocity changes, text displacement, masked reveals. Strict rule: every move maps to effort, strike, or recovery.

**Interaction ideas**  
- Scroll “set counter” in the margin  
- Tap/hold on mobile to “lock in” a training path  
- Cursor/touch leaves a short directional trail only on arena pages (subtle, disable for reduced-motion)

**Why it fits**  
Makes “movement translated into digital design” literal. Highly differentiated from hospitality and template gym sites.

**Risks**  
- Highest performance cost; easy to ship laggy on mid-range Android  
- Can sacrifice clarity for spectacle  
- Harder for non-design clients to approve from static comps  
- May feel too “global tech-sport” and less rooted in Manjeri community

## E. RECOMMENDED DIRECTION

### Recommendation: **Direction 02 — ONE PHILOSOPHY / TWO ARENAS**  
executed with the **editorial craft system of Direction 01**  
and **motion discipline borrowed from Direction 03** (selective, not scroll-theatre everywhere).

### Why this wins

1. **It is true to the business.** Dual arenas are not a creative stunt; they are the offer.  
2. **It cannot be confused with prior studio work** (no cream hospitality, no wellness spa, no SaaS card stack).  
3. **It cannot be confused with generic gym templates** (those never centre fencing/combat as equal).  
4. **It creates ownable DNA:** even without the logo, the dual-arena structure + black/maroon editorial system should read as Beyond Fitness.  
5. **It still answers local UX needs** if we keep utilitarian paths (WhatsApp, map, hours, start) always available.

### What we deliberately leave behind

- Pure Kinetic Field as the master concept (too fragile for mobile-first local traffic)  
- Pure Dossier alone (excellent craft, weaker strategic uniqueness)

### Working creative title

**BEYOND — TWO ARENAS. ONE STANDARD.**

## F. DESIGN DNA

Provisional system for the recommended direction. Final tokens refine after logo + client colour confirmation.

### Typography philosophy

| Role | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Display | Condensed athletic grotesque — wide statements, tight leading |
| UI / body | Neutral neo-grotesque — high legibility on mobile |
| Meta | Monospace or small caps for labels, times, arena tags |

Rules:

- One idea per headline  
- Prefer short verbs: TRAIN / STRIKE / BUILD / ADVANCE  
- No motivational poster poetry  
- Numbers earn size only when they mean something (hours, years, disciplines — not fake stats)

### Colour philosophy

| Token | Intent |
|-------|--------|
| Void / near-black | Primary ground — seriousness |
| Bone / off-white | Primary text & reverse ground |
| Graphite | Secondary surfaces |
| Maroon / gym-red | Accent of heat & impact only |
| Signal white lines | Structure, lanes, cuts |

Avoid: neon, purple gradients, gold luxury accents, cream wellness fields, rainbow fitness energy.

### Spacing

- Sections breathe like rest between sets: generous vertical rhythm  
- Dense clusters only when listing training disciplines  
- Mobile: fewer simultaneous ideas; one focal image or one statement

### Grid

- Desktop: asymmetric 12-column editorial (often 7/5 or 8/4, not equal 6/6)  
- Tablet: collapse to stacked editorial with retained overhangs  
- Mobile: single column with intentional full-bleed breaks  
- Occasional full-bleed “arena threshold” that ignores the content grid on purpose

### Image treatment

- High contrast, slightly matte  
- Aggressive crops  
- Minimal rounded corners (0–4px max)  
- Optional subtle grain  
- Prefer sequences over collages  
- Never floating polaroid / glass image cards

### Shapes

- Sharp rectangles, hard edges  
- Diagonal only when communicating combat/speed  
- No blobs, no soft organic shapes

### Borders

- Hairline rules (1px) as structure  
- Thick bars sparingly as “impact” separators  
- No heavy card outlines everywhere

### Buttons

- Rectangular / slight radius (≤4px)  
- Primary: solid light-on-dark or maroon fill  
- Secondary: outline  
- Labels: `Start training`, `WhatsApp`, `Get directions`, `Talk to a coach` — not `Join Now` / `Transform`  
- No glow, no gradient pills

### Cards

- Default: **no cards**  
- Cards only when interaction requires a container (e.g. selecting a training path, FAQ)  
- If a border/background isn’t needed for understanding, remove it

### Navigation

- Minimal: Training / Arenas (or Disciplines) / Coaches / Visit / Start  
- Persistent WhatsApp affordance on mobile  
- Arena switcher can appear in nav as a quiet dual control once past first viewport  
- Avoid mega-menus

### Motion

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---------|-----------|
| Masked image reveals | Bounce / jelly |
| Directional page/section wipes between arenas | Random parallax layers |
| Type entering with weight | Auto-playing hero video walls |
| Hover crop shifts | Glow pulses |
| Scroll-linked progress marker | Scroll-jacking long sequences |

Duration: decisive (180–450ms). Heavier moments up to ~700ms. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Hover behaviour

- Desktop: image crop nudge, underline/rule extend, button fill snap  
- Never rely on hover for essential info  
- Touch: press states (opacity/scale micro) instead

### Mobile behaviour

- First screen: brand + one line of meaning + primary CTA (WhatsApp / Start) + one dominant real image  
- Dual arenas introduced early but as a clear chooser, not a complex interaction  
- Sticky bottom utility bar optional: WhatsApp · Call · Map  
- Motion simplified; no multi-layer parallax  
- Type scales aggressively but never becomes illegible fashion type

## G. INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Structure from the business, not a template.

### Proposed sitemap

```
Home
├── The Standard          (what Beyond Fitness is / philosophy)
├── Training Arenas
│   ├── Fitness           (gym, PT, conditioning, nutrition)
│   └── Combat & Sport    (fencing, kickboxing, martial arts…)
├── Disciplines           (flat index of all trainables — confirm list with client)
├── Coaches / People
├── Visit                 (address, hours, map, parking/mall context)
├── Start                 (how to begin — trial / WhatsApp / call)
└── Legal / Privacy       (minimal)
```

Optional later: Schedules, Memberships/Pricing, Gallery, Youth/Fencing for parents.

### Homepage narrative (not template order)

1. **Identity hit** — brand + one sentence: training culture, not just a gym  
2. **Dual arenas introduction** — Fitness / Combat & Sport as equal doors  
3. **Proof of place** — real interiors, real people, Manjeri rooted  
4. **Who it’s for** — short paths (body / skill / youth / coaching)  
5. **How to start** — frictionless next step  
6. **Visit strip** — map, hours, WhatsApp (always findable)

Testimonials only if real and specific. Pricing only when client confirms strategy (many local users prefer WhatsApp for price).

### Content principles

- Confirm exact service list before locking Disciplines  
- Prefer verbs and training nouns over marketing adjectives  
- Malayalam consideration later if client wants bilingual — design system should allow it without breaking type

## H. SIGNATURE INTERACTIONS

2–3 memorable interactions that earn their place:

### 1. Arena Threshold
A single scroll or click moment where the page divides into **FITNESS** and **COMBAT / SPORT**, then reunites under *One standard.*  
**Why:** Encodes the business model in one unforgettable beat.

### 2. Discipline Index (physical list)
A typographic index of disciplines that expands into photography on hover/tap — like calling out training stations.  
**Why:** Answers “What can I train here?” with swagger, not cards.

### 3. Start Protocol
A short, linear “how to begin” (3 steps) with WhatsApp as the primary action — feels like being handed a starter plan, not a lead-gen form.  
**Why:** Converts Instagram/mobile traffic without killing the premium tone.

## J. IMPLEMENTATION PLAN

### Repository reality check

The `BEYOND FITNESS` project folder is currently **empty** (greenfield).  
There is no existing framework, component library, or CSS architecture to inherit inside this folder.

### Recommended stack (aligned with recent studio Next projects)

| Layer | Choice | Why |
|-------|--------|-----|
| Framework | **Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript** | Matches Al Mahmasani / Novo / Defau Studio; strong image + routing |
| Styling | **Tailwind CSS v4 + CSS variables** | Speed with custom DNA via tokens — not default Tailwind look |
| Motion | **Framer Motion** first | Already used in studio stack; sufficient for arena transitions & reveals |
| Extra motion | **GSAP + ScrollTrigger only if needed** | Add only for complex scroll choreography; avoid by default |
| Images | **`next/image` + disciplined asset pipeline** | Real photography will make or break this brand |
| Icons | Minimal custom/SVG — avoid Lucide icon salad | Sports brands don’t need 40 outline icons |
| Forms / contact | WhatsApp deep links + simple enquiry | Local conversion reality |
| Deploy | Vercel or static export as per hosting preference | Confirm with client |

### What we will not do technically

- Do not install a UI kit and “theme it into a gym”  
- Do not start from a Tailwind UI marketing template  
- Do not add Three.js / R3F unless a future phase truly needs it (it doesn’t for v1)  
- Do not ship heavy scroll libraries on day one

### Build phases (after approval)

1. **Approve direction** (this document)  
2. **Collect assets** — logo, interior photos, combat photos, trainer portraits, brand red reference  
3. **Lock tokens** — type pairing, colour values, spacing scale  
4. **Content confirmation** — exact disciplines, coaches, hours, CTAs  
5. **Wireframe the Arena IA** (low fidelity)  
6. **Design key screens** — mobile first: Home, Arena Fitness, Arena Combat, Start, Visit  
7. **Scaffold Next app** in this repo  
8. **Implement section by section** with motion system constraints  
9. **Performance pass** on mid-range mobile  
10. **Client review → refine → launch**

### Success criteria

Someone landing from Instagram on a phone should immediately understand:

> This is Beyond Fitness in Manjeri — a serious place to train body and skill — and I know how to start (WhatsApp).

Someone who has seen our previous hospitality/wellness sites should feel:

> This is a completely different design DNA.
